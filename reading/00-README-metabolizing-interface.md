# The Metabolizing Interface — reading list

Opened 2026-08-21 for outline V.3d (Matthew's component, wording preserved
there): as digital interfaces become inherently intelligent, we gain the
option to shape every bit that reaches us; the isolation of masters —
spiritual and technological (Knuth; possibly Dijkstra) — reveals a
contamination worth heavy cost to control; the returned master's capacity is
what the interface should be — **transmutation of the perceived-negative
through complete understanding of its origins, as opposed to quarantine,
which requires some intelligence in the line to suffer it.**

Siblings: `user-modeling/` (the OUTBOUND half — emission control conditioned
on the user model; this directory is reception), `the-trustworthy-firm/`
(who controls the transmuter = its governance question), `model-behavior/`
(whether a machine in the line suffers = the Kim/welfare thread).

## The five clusters

**1. Reappraisal vs suppression — the measured form of transmutation vs
quarantine.** Gross's emotion-regulation process model and its meta-analytic
record: antecedent-focused reappraisal (change the meaning before the
response forms) beats response-focused suppression on affect, memory, and
social outcomes. The design claim is reappraisal implemented in the
interface rather than trained into the person.

**2. The isolation record — retreat and return.** Knuth's no-email statement
(primary, his own page); Dijkstra's habits (VERIFY status); the
contemplative retreat-and-return structure with Plato's return to the cave
as the canonical Western form; Simon's "wealth of information creates a
poverty of attention" as the economic statement of the contamination.

**3. AI-mediated communication — the experiments' live field.** Hancock's
research agenda; Sharma's measured empathic rewriting (Nature MI); the
Habermas machine (Science 2024 — AI mediation beating human mediators on
group approval); Hohenstein's suspected-AI-degrades-trust (the design
constraint: the receiver's instrument, never the sender's covert polish);
smart-reply sentiment effects.

**4. Bubbles and their empirics — the objection cluster.** Nguyen's
epistemic-bubbles/echo-chambers distinction; Pariser's filter bubble; and
the empirical record that complicates the fear (Bakshy 2015; the 2023
Meta/Science studies; Flaxman-Goel-Rao) — carried per convention, since the
transmutation design must beat BOTH the bubble objection and the
weak-bubble-effects literature that would make the objection lazy.

**5. The quarantine cost — moderation's sufferers.** Commercial content
moderation as quarantine run on human intelligences: Roberts's fieldwork,
the moderator-wellbeing literature, the 2020 settlement record. Grounds the
sharpest clause of the claim and hands the model-welfare thread its testable
design property (does transmutation-by-understanding avoid RELOCATING the
suffering?).

### (c) Reading path for V.3d (38 steps; ★★ marked *skippable* on a first pass)

*The measured distinction — quarantine's three-channel price, then its own objections (8)*
1. **Gross 1998** — transmute vs quarantine, measured on one person: reappraisal cuts the emotion, suppression only hides it and charges the body.
2. **Richards & Gross 2000** + **Butler 2003** — the other two channels of the same bill. Suppression costs *memory* for what actually happened (reappraisal costs nothing on the same measures), and it costs the **uninformed partner's** blood pressure and the rapport between them. Body, memory, partner: that is quarantine priced in full, and Butler's reappraisal arm carries none of it. Butler is an image-only scan — verify any quoted magnitude visually.
3. **Webb 2012** — the meta-analytic decomposition. Attentional deployment (quarantine's family) is d = 0.00; reframing the *stimulus* beats reframing one's *response*. This is the taxonomy that keeps the preference from being a slogan.
4. **Uusberg 2023** — and this is the taxonomy one level down. Reconstrual (change the construal of the situation) vs repurposing (change the goals it is evaluated against); object-level vs meta-level. V.3d's verb is *reconstrual*, and the distinction is exactly the line between re-describing the incoming bit and quietly re-weighting what the receiver wants.
5. **Troy 2013** — the sign flip: successful reappraisal hurts when the situation was controllable. Read before you believe step 1. Then **Ford & Troy 2019**, the objection as a framework: hedonic vs instrumental cost, and the identity paradox.
6. **Raio 2013** — the mechanism fails the stress test. Trained reappraisal produces nothing under acute stress, i.e. exactly when it was bought. This kills "teach the person to reframe" and is therefore the shelf's strongest positive argument for the transmuter being *in the line*: an interface has no stress-impaired dorsolateral PFC. Whether an externally supplied reappraisal also fails under stress is untested — that is V.3d's to argue.
7. **Feinberg 2020** — and the cost of *success*. Reappraising guilt and shame raises job satisfaction and causally raises counterproductive behaviour toward the counterparty. In Yang's own domain. The argument for an explicit exception for action-signalling negative affect. (Note the correction: guilt and shame, not anger.)
8. *skippable* **Riepenhausen 2022** — the affirmative base, so steps 3–7 do not read as pure deflation. (**Ford, Gross & Gruber 2019** for why every effect above is a component estimate pulled from a blend; **Wang 2021** for scale and the preregistered null; **Denny 2014** for the inconvenient finding that the *trainable* tactic is distancing; **Gross & John 2003** for the instrument.)

*The isolation record — why controlling reception was ever worth heavy cost (3)*
9. **Knuth** — the primary renunciation, and note the secretary: the quarantine works because a human absorbs what it refuses.
10. **Dijkstra EWD1305** then **OH 330** — the doctrine (unlearning is impossible) and the bracketed clause about not being required to suffer the diseases you study, then the thesis stated outright with conformity pressure named as the contaminant.
11. **Simon 1971** — the economics: the cost of information is borne by the recipient. This is the warrant for spending heavily on the interface. (*skippable texture:* Migliorati, Newport, EWD1000, Schutte & Dreyer.)

*The bridge from the process model to interface design (1)*
12. **Slovák 2023** — read before any built system. The strategic axis — "offline" skill training vs "on-the-spot" in-situ support — is reappraisal-trained-into-the-person vs reappraisal-implemented-in-the-interface, taxonomised by Gross himself, and its survey names the empty slot the next six steps fill. Carry the honest tension: the field's default frame is developing an embodied skill in the person, which is the alternative V.3d rejects. (*skippable* **Kitson 2024** — 42 interventions, every one of them training the person, with the missing in-the-moment AI support stated as the field's own gap. Cite it so the design claim reads as extending an agenda rather than asserting novelty.)

*AI-mediated communication — the built artefacts, ending on the prior art (7)*
13. **Hancock 2020** — the vocabulary, the sender/receiver axis, and the ossification objection from the field's own founder.
14. **Sharma (HAILEY)** — transmutation in flight, in an RCT, with the human keeping the channel.
15. **Sharma 2023 (ACL)** — **the specification.** Reframing rather than removing, defined over seven measurable linguistic attributes, tested on 2,067 real users: empathic reframes preferred 55.7% more, specific 43.1% more, and *highly positive ones 22.7% less*. Brightening every incoming bit is measurably worse than reframing it specifically — that is the difference between quarantine-by-sweetening and metabolizing, in a number. Sender-side self-help, so the interpersonal channel is untested here.
16. **Sharma 2024 (CHI)** — the same lab at scale: 15,531 people in the wild, 67% emotional-intensity reduction, and the finding nobody else supplies — the transmutation is **not uniform across receivers**, working worst for adolescents, males and lower-education users. An interface that reframes for everyone reframes best for the already-articulate. (*skippable* **Zhan 2024** for whether the output is the Gross construct at all, and for the 7B result that lets the transmuter run locally; **Morris 2015** and **Smith 2021** for the pre-LLM crowd-powered versions — Panoply's between-groups null on depression is the calibrating result, and Flip\*Doubt's failure taxonomy is the rubric.)
17. **Argyle 2023** — field scale, and the effect lands on the **partner**, not the tool's user. The topic analysis is the transmutation/quarantine distinction made empirical: nothing filtered, no argument removed, the same claims arriving in a form the other side could receive — and policy attitudes did not move. Sender-side architecture, receiver-side effect.
18. **Das Swain 2025 (Care-Pilot)** — the closest operational analogue: a model absorbing hostile client contact for the worker who currently absorbs it, i.e. cluster 5's problem answered by transmutation rather than attenuation. The mechanism the workers report is *humanizing the client* — understanding the abuse's origins. And its honest limit: they rated the machine's empathy above their coworkers' and still called shared experience indispensable.
19. **Yang et al., CHI '26** — **stop here and read closely.** The interface V.3d posits, built and measured on the receiver: it works on affect, does nothing to behaviour, and silently edits the reader's construal of the power relation.

*The disclosure squeeze — a named section of V.3d, not a caveat (5)*
20. **Jakesch 2023**, then its scaled preregistered successor **Williams-Ceci & Jakesch 2026** (*Sci. Adv.*; the on-disk filename handle "fisher" is legacy). An intelligence with a position, sitting in the line, edits the person and not just the message — and the participants most shifted were the least able to detect it, so self-report is foreclosed as the audit channel. Worse: warning them beforehand and debriefing them afterward both failed. Read against step 17: Argyle changed tone and left attitudes alone, this changed attitudes. The pair localizes what is safe to build.
21. **Yin 2024** + **Rubin 2025** — the penalty, decomposed and then isolated. Yin separates the response effect (+0.57) from the label effect (−0.68): independent, additive, roughly equal, so the cost attaches to being *known* to metabolize. Rubin holds the text constant across nine studies and shows the erosion triggers on mere **suspicion** of assistance — an auditable transmuter pays by construction and an unauditable one survives only until it is guessed at. Rubin's Study 3 is the escape hatch and should be read as a scoping rule: in a purely cognitive register the gap closes. Transmute construal; do not attempt to supply feeling-with.
22. **Schilke & Reimann 2025** — the same result from organizational behaviour, 13 experiments, every moderator tried, and undisclosed-then-exposed worse still, so "just don't disclose" is not an exit either. The disclosing party is always the sender; the receiver-side version of this design is arguably the single most useful experiment V.3d could commission.
23. **Bruns 2016/2018** — the wedge, and the exit from the corner. Disclosing the nudge's *purpose* preserved the effect; disclosing *that you are influencing* is the part that wobbled. That is a concrete interface requirement: publish the transmuter's purpose and its reframing rule, not a badge saying the bit was touched. (*skippable* **Mariadassou 2024** — the aversion attaches to the undifferentiated word "AI," which makes auditability a craft problem; **Ovsyannikova 2025** — an advantage over trained experts large enough to survive disclosure; **Liu 2022** — the same squeeze in V.3d's exact email channel, where the penalty tracks the *salience* of mediation rather than its presence; **Hohenstein 2023** with **Hohenstein & Jung 2020** as a pair, the latter for where blame lands.)

*What the machine itself breaks, how it wears out, and who games it (3)*
24. **Moore 2025** — the failure mode quarantine does not have: a filter that drops a message cannot invent a worse one, but a model in the emotional line emits stigma and sycophantically endorses delusions. Read against steps 21 and 23 — third parties find machine compassion superior in single written exchanges, and that superiority does not survive acute clinical stakes. Both true; the gap between them is where the design lives, and it draws the boundary V.3d must defend: receiver-side framing of a message, never provider substitution.
25. **Vance 2017** — durability. Attention collapses after the *second* exposure and keeps falling across five days, measured neurally; the 24-hour rest does not offset it. A fixed reframe becomes wallpaper, and quarantine-you-have-learned-to-ignore is what a degraded transmuter is. The design answer is inside the same paper: polymorphism held up, which turns "complete understanding of its origins" into load-bearing engineering — the reframe must vary with each message, not emit a house style.
26. **Kamenica & Gentzkow 2011** with **Hardt 2016** — the adversarial-reception outsiders, and the reason auditability is not free. A *fully known* transformation rule still moves a rational Bayesian receiver and the sender captures the gain: publishing the rule does not neutralize it, it defines the target. Hardt gives the same intuition a cost model and the boundary — robust under separable manipulation costs, NP-hard in general. Neither concerns emotion; porting them is the contribution, not the citation. (*skippable* **Dalvi 2004** for the same claim in the currency of perpetual manual reconstruction — quarantine's maintenance bill.)

*The bubble objections, with the empirics that keep them honest (5)*
27. **Nguyen 2020** — why pushing different bits is the wrong lever; the pathology is in trust, not exposure.
28. **Ross Arguedas 2022** + **Nyhan 2023** — the containing synthesis and the precisely-estimated null: insulation is self-selected rather than imposed, and the exposure layer exists but is not where the effect lives.
29. **Guess 2023 (feed algorithms)** + **Budak 2024** — composition change is not reception change, and Budak supplies the denominators that make the point unarguable (IRA content at 0.004% of the feed; 1% of users taking 70% of exposures). Two consequences for V.3d: the design cannot be motivated by rescuing the median receiver from toxic bits, and Budak's four-way taxonomy of overstated exposure is the checklist to run against V.3d's own harm claims before they are made. The warrant moves to the tails and to second-person channels — email, DMs, review, the queue.
30. **Eslami 2015** — the invisible edit, measured. 62.5% did not know the feed hid anything, and users blamed *their friends* for what the algorithm removed: an undisclosed transmuter does not merely alter a message, it rewrites the reader's model of the sender, which is precisely what Yang found at step 19. The follow-up is the constructive half and the counterweight to step 22 — making the edit visible cost nothing in satisfaction and bought engagement and felt control.
31. **Piccardi 2025** — the positive result the weak-effects synthesis has to accommodate, and the shape of V.3d's claim in the field: an LLM reranking animosity in *live* feeds, installed by the receiver without platform permission, moving out-party feeling in ten days. Where Guess changed composition and the person did not move, Piccardi changed what construals were salient and the person did. Read the mechanism honestly — this is salience management, not reinterpretation, and only the one attitude moved. (*skippable* **Liu 2025** for the tightest bound yet on induced bubbles; **Sourati 2026** for the homogenization objection restated for the AI-mediation era, which the exposure nulls do not touch; **Bakshy 2015**, **Flaxman 2016**, **Guess 2023 (reshares)**, **Pariser 2011**.)

*Quarantine's sufferers, ending on the receiver's own refusal (7)*
32. **Roberts 2016** — the filter's invisibility is the product; users cannot locate the interface shaping them.
33. **Scola settlement (2020)** — §5.1.5. The contamination premise priced at $52M, and the toolkit is *attenuation*: blur, grayscale, mute. (Final-approval order for the number and the class.)
34. **Newton 2019** — the floor itself, and epistemic capture passing through the filter into the filterer.
35. **Woodhams & Duran 2024** — how the exposure becomes the injury, and the account routes straight back through cluster 1: suppression and rumination maintain the symptomatology, reappraisal and acceptance attenuate it. This is the bridge that lets the cost-relocation test be stated as a mechanism rather than an analogy. A Perspective — no pathway here is a tested effect.
36. **Steiger 2021 §6.2** + **Das 2020** — the existing engineering practice and exactly where it stops, then that gap measured: interactive control preserves accuracy and the wellbeing benefit does not reach significance. Filtering the pixels is not metabolizing the content.
37. **Jhaver 2023** — the same bill handed to the ordinary receiver. Give users a quarantine dial and the moderator's exposure burden becomes the user's *configuration* burden, which they decline to pay ("this is supposed to be fun!") and which several call unpaid training data. And the constraint that separates V.3d from Pariser's invisible edit: users refused filters because they wanted to hear others out. A filter demands you specify in advance what you cannot yet see; transmutation-by-understanding-origins requires no such specification. Stated preference, n = 24.
38. *skippable* **Qian 2026** — the harm relocated into AI-development labelling, and the honest case *against* warning the receiver. (**Chen 2022** for assume-don't-ask as the consent principle; **Gorwa 2020** for automating quarantine burying the politics; **Pendse 2025** for de-roling as the human analogue of transmutation.)

### (d) Gaps — what arrived, what narrowed, what V.3d still has to build

**Answered on disk.**

1. **The absent primaries (old GAP 6).** Closed. `richards-gross-2000-emotion-regulation-and-memory.pdf` and `butler-2003-social-consequences-expressive-suppression.pdf` are both here, and between them they complete quarantine's price in three channels: body (Gross 1998), memory (Richards & Gross), partner (Butler). Butler is the more valuable arrival — the *uninformed* partner's blood pressure rises, which converts "quarantine requires some intelligence in the line to suffer it" from metaphor into a measurable design property: the right question to ask of any interface holding material back is whose blood pressure moves. Butler is an image-only scan; OCR or read visually. **Leroy 2009 (attention residue) is still not on disk** — identified with a DOI, never fetched; it is the last of the old list.
2. **The construct itself.** `uusberg-2023-reappraising-reappraisal-expanded-view.pdf` gives V.3d's central verb moving parts — reconstrual vs repurposing, object-level vs meta-level — and `slovak-2023-designing-emotion-regulation-interventions-arxiv.pdf` (cite TOCHI 30(1):13, never the preprint's placeholder volume) gives the design-side vocabulary in Gross's own co-authorship: "offline" training vs "on-the-spot" in-situ support. `kitson-2024-supporting-cognitive-reappraisal-digital-technology.pdf` surveys 42 interventions and finds all of them training the person, then names the empty slot. Between them the design claim can be stated as an extension of a live agenda rather than an assertion — Matthew's build-on-published-work convention, satisfied.
3. **Auditability and the disclosure problem (old GAP 2/4).** Answered richly, uncomfortably, and from four disjoint literatures, which is why it should be a named section rather than a caveat. `eslami-2015-invisible-algorithms-news-feeds.pdf` is the empirical anchor Pariser's invisible-edit constraint never had — and its constructive half (revelation cost nothing in satisfaction, bought engagement and felt control) is the only on-disk evidence that auditable transmutation is possible. Against it: `jakesch-2023-co-writing-acm-vor.pdf` and `fisher-2026-biased-ai-writing-assistants.pdf` (Williams-Ceci & Jakesch — the filename is a legacy handle), where the shifted are the least able to detect the shift and warnings do not mitigate; `yin-2024-ai-help-people-feel-heard.pdf`, which decomposes the penalty into orthogonal response and label effects; `rubin-2025-perceived-human-vs-ai-empathy-preprint.pdf`, where mere *suspicion* of assistance is enough; and `schilke-2025-transparency-dilemma-ai-disclosure-accepted-osf.pdf`, 13 experiments deep with no surviving escape via non-disclosure. The wedges: `bruns-2016-can-nudges-be-transparent-and-yet-effective-wp-draft.pdf` (disclose the *purpose*, not the fact of influence — publish the rule, not a badge), `mariadassou-2024-averse-to-what-algorithmic-labels.pdf` (the penalty rides the undifferentiated word "AI"), `ovsyannikova-2025-ai-more-compassionate-than-expert-humans.pdf` (a margin large enough to survive disclosure), and Rubin's Study 3 (in a purely cognitive register the gap closes — transmute construal, do not supply feeling-with).
4. **The affirmative counterweight to Webb.** `riepenhausen-2022-positive-cognitive-reappraisal-review.pdf` plus `ford-2019-emotion-polyregulation.pdf`: the reappraisal preference can now be carried as supported rather than defended, and the d = 0.00 null is correctly read as a component estimate pulled from a blend.
5. **Receiver-side consent, priced (old GAP 5's first half).** `jhaver-2023-personalizing-content-moderation.pdf` is the best answer on disk: hand the receiver a quarantine dial and the moderator's exposure burden becomes the user's configuration burden, which users decline to pay — and some refuse filters outright because they want to hear others out. That is the wanting-to-be-contaminated result and the strongest available argument for transmutation over personalizable filtering. `chen-2022-trauma-informed-computing.pdf` supplies the principle that keeps it from collapsing into interrogation: assume the possibility of harm rather than requiring disclosure of vulnerability.
6. **The mechanism under the suffering-in-the-line clause.** `woodhams-2024-secondary-traumatic-stress-analytical-staff.pdf` routes exposure-to-injury through exactly the constructs cluster 1 measures — suppression and rumination maintain, reappraisal and acceptance attenuate — which is the bridge from cluster 5 back to cluster 1. A Perspective; no pathway in it is a tested effect.

**Narrowed, not closed.**

7. **Habituation and longitudinal reception (GAP 1).** Narrowed hard from two sides and still open in the middle. `vance-2017-habituation-to-warnings.pdf` is the sharpest objection available — attention collapses after the *second* exposure, keeps falling across five days, and polymorphic warnings resist it, which is the design answer (vary with the message's actual origins; do not emit a house style) inside the same paper. `denny-2014-behavioral-effects-longitudinal-training-cognitive-reappraisal.pdf` supplies the human-side answer and it is inconvenient: the tactic that trains, generalizes and carries into daily life is **distancing**, which sits nearer quarantine than reinterpretation. `sharma-2024-self-guided-cognitive-restructuring.pdf` bites it partially — repeated real use across a large deployment — but privacy rules prevent session linkage, so there is still no per-person longitudinal record. **Still open: nothing measures a year of reframed real interpersonal messages on one receiver.**
8. **Displacement (GAP 5's second half) — still the directory's named empirical hole.** No study on disk or in the sweep measures whether putting a model in the line reduces the human absorber's exposure or harm. `dasswain-2025-ai-on-my-shoulder.pdf` is the closest — a model absorbing hostile client contact for the worker — but its evidence grades downward across three studies and it measures no wellbeing, burnout or turnover outcome at all, and its interviews follow a *simulation*, not live contact. Qian 2026 and Pendse 2025 remain relocation proxies. Treat the emptiness as the finding, and note the shape of the missing study is now clear: Care-Pilot's design with an absorber-harm endpoint.

**Contribution sites — build, do not acquire.**

9. **Adversarial reception (GAP 3).** Answered only from outside the field, and the outside is now well stocked: `kamenica-gentzkow-2011-bayesian-persuasion.pdf` (a *fully known* transformation rule still moves a rational Bayesian receiver, and the sender captures the gain — publishing the reframing rule does not neutralize it, it defines the target), `hardt-2016-strategic-classification.pdf` (the cost model, with robustness efficient under separable costs and NP-hard in general), `dalvi-2004-adversarial-classification.pdf` (the same claim in the currency of perpetual manual reconstruction). None concerns emotion, meaning, or human receivers. **No work applies strategic-classification or Bayesian-persuasion reasoning to an emotion-reframing receiver interface.** V.3d must supply the missing piece itself: a cost model for manipulating an emotional framing. Note the sharpest form of the problem, which these three make unavoidable — auditability (item 3) and gameability are the *same property*, so the act that makes the transmuter trustworthy is the act that makes it a target.
10. **The longitudinal outcome variable, now the primary endpoint.** The cost-accounting the new objections force — Feinberg's causal counterproductive behaviour, Raio's stress-contingency, Vance's habituation curve, Sourati's population-level convergence — has changed what V.3d has to measure. Momentary affect is no longer the endpoint; Riepenhausen supplies the right vocabulary (does the interface move *resilience-related outcomes*, i.e. moderate the stressor-to-outcome relation over time), and Yang's own limitation — affect moved, behaviour did not — is the gap in the same words. Nobody has run it. **This is the single experiment the shelf now most obviously demands.**
11. **The receiver-side disclosure experiment.** Schilke & Reimann's disclosing party is always the sender or an evaluated actor; Yin's, Rubin's and Liu 2022's are message authors. Not one study puts the disclosed AI on the *reader's* side of the wire, which is the only configuration V.3d proposes. The transfer is currently an inference. Running Schilke's design with the instrument owned by the receiver is cheap, and it is the experiment that decides whether the squeeze applies at all.
12. **Whether a machine in the line suffers.** Unchanged: the model-welfare half lives in the sibling `model-behavior/` directory, not here, and V.3d's clause depends on it. Das Swain and Hohenstein & Jung 2020 (the crumple zone — the machine credited with nothing on success and charged on failure) make the question concrete rather than rhetorical.
