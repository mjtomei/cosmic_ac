# The Metabolizing Interface — reading list

Opened 2026-08-21 for outline V.3d (Matthew's component, wording preserved
there): as digital interfaces become inherently intelligent, we gain the
option to shape every bit that reaches us; the isolation of masters —
spiritual and technological (Knuth; possibly Dijkstra) — reveals a
contamination worth heavy cost to control; the returned master's capacity is
what the interface should be — **transmutation of the perceived-negative
through complete understanding of its origins, as opposed to quarantine,
which requires some intelligence in the line to suffer it.**

Siblings: `user-modeling/` (the OUTBOUND half — emission control conditioned
on the user model; this directory is reception), `the-trustworthy-firm/`
(who controls the transmuter = its governance question), `model-behavior/`
(whether a machine in the line suffers = the Kim/welfare thread).

## The five clusters

**1. Reappraisal vs suppression — the measured form of transmutation vs
quarantine.** Gross's emotion-regulation process model and its meta-analytic
record: antecedent-focused reappraisal (change the meaning before the
response forms) beats response-focused suppression on affect, memory, and
social outcomes. The design claim is reappraisal implemented in the
interface rather than trained into the person.

**2. The isolation record — retreat and return.** Knuth's no-email statement
(primary, his own page); Dijkstra's habits (VERIFY status); the
contemplative retreat-and-return structure with Plato's return to the cave
as the canonical Western form; Simon's "wealth of information creates a
poverty of attention" as the economic statement of the contamination.

**3. AI-mediated communication — the experiments' live field.** Hancock's
research agenda; Sharma's measured empathic rewriting (Nature MI); the
Habermas machine (Science 2024 — AI mediation beating human mediators on
group approval); Hohenstein's suspected-AI-degrades-trust (the design
constraint: the receiver's instrument, never the sender's covert polish);
smart-reply sentiment effects.

**4. Bubbles and their empirics — the objection cluster.** Nguyen's
epistemic-bubbles/echo-chambers distinction; Pariser's filter bubble; and
the empirical record that complicates the fear (Bakshy 2015; the 2023
Meta/Science studies; Flaxman-Goel-Rao) — carried per convention, since the
transmutation design must beat BOTH the bubble objection and the
weak-bubble-effects literature that would make the objection lazy.

**5. The quarantine cost — moderation's sufferers.** Commercial content
moderation as quarantine run on human intelligences: Roberts's fieldwork,
the moderator-wellbeing literature, the 2020 settlement record. Grounds the
sharpest clause of the claim and hands the model-welfare thread its testable
design property (does transmutation-by-understanding avoid RELOCATING the
suffering?).

### (c) Reading path for V.3d (26 steps; ★★ marked *skippable* on a first pass)

*The measured distinction — with its own objections attached (5)*
1. **Gross 1998** — transmute vs quarantine, measured on one person: reappraisal cuts the emotion, suppression only hides it and charges the body.
2. **Webb 2012** — the meta-analytic decomposition. Attentional deployment (quarantine's family) is d = 0.00; reframing the *stimulus* beats reframing one's *response*. This is the taxonomy that keeps the preference from being a slogan.
3. **Troy 2013** — the sign flip: successful reappraisal hurts when the situation was controllable. Read before you believe step 1.
4. **Ford & Troy 2019** — the objection as a framework; hedonic vs instrumental cost, and the identity paradox.
5. *skippable* **Wang 2021** — scale, honest deflation of effect sizes, and the preregistered null on blunted action.

*The isolation record — why controlling reception was ever worth heavy cost (5)*
6. **Knuth** — the primary renunciation, and note the secretary: the quarantine works because a human absorbs what it refuses.
7. **Dijkstra EWD1305** — the doctrine (unlearning is impossible) and the bracketed clause about not being required to suffer the disease you study.
8. **Dijkstra OH 330** — the thesis stated outright, with conformity pressure named as the contaminant.
9. **Simon 1971** — the economics: the cost of information is borne by the recipient. This is the warrant for spending heavily on the interface.
10. *skippable* **Migliorati 2021** — withdrawal as tactical, for the sake of return. (Newport, EWD1000, Schutte & Dreyer are optional texture.)

*AI-mediated communication — the field, ending on the prior art (4)*
11. **Hancock 2020** — the vocabulary, the sender/receiver axis, and the ossification objection from the field's own founder.
12. **Sharma (HAILEY)** — transmutation in flight, in an RCT, with the human keeping the channel.
13. *skippable but recommended* **Hohenstein 2023** — the disclosure hazard: the penalty attaches to being known to metabolize, not to metabolizing. (Mieczkowski for the adjacency-pair gap; Tessler §§4–5 for group scale and manipulability.)
14. **Yang et al., CHI '26** — **stop here and read closely.** The interface V.3d posits, built and measured on the receiver: it works on affect, does nothing to behaviour, and silently edits the reader's construal of the power relation.

*The bubble objections, with the empirics that keep them honest (5)*
15. **Nguyen 2020** — why pushing different bits is the wrong lever; the pathology is in trust, not exposure.
16. **Ross Arguedas 2022** — the containing synthesis: the bubble effect the popular case assumes is not there.
17. **Nyhan 2023** — the exposure layer exists and is not where the effect lives.
18. **Guess 2023 (feed algorithms)** — composition change is not reception change; note the general-equilibrium hedge, which is V.3d's escape hatch and its unfinished business.
19. *skippable* **Bakshy 2015** / **Flaxman 2016** / **Guess 2023 (reshares)** — the decomposition, the off-platform denominator, and a crude quarantine priced in lost knowledge. **Pariser 2011** for the invisible-edit constraint.

*Quarantine's sufferers, ending on the displacement gap (7)*
20. **Roberts 2016** — the filter's invisibility is the product; users cannot locate the interface shaping them.
21. **Scola settlement (2020)** — §5.1.5. The contamination premise priced at $52M, and the toolkit is *attenuation*: blur, grayscale, mute. (Final-approval order for the number and the class.)
22. **Newton 2019** — the floor itself, and epistemic capture passing through the filter into the filterer.
23. **Steiger 2021 §6.2** — the existing engineering practice, and exactly where it stops. The gap between the blur slider and the metabolizing interface is the argument.
24. **Das 2020** — that gap, measured: interactive control preserves accuracy, and the wellbeing benefit does not reach significance. Filtering the pixels is not metabolizing the content.
25. **Qian 2026** — the harm relocated into AI-development labelling; and the honest case *against* warning the receiver.
26. *skippable* **Gorwa 2020** — automating quarantine buries the politics rather than dissolving them. **Pendse 2025** for de-roling as the human analogue of transmutation.

### (d) Gaps — what V.3d still lacks an on-disk anchor for

1. **The displacement question, still unmeasured.** No study on disk (or found in the sweep) measures whether automated moderation actually reduced human exposure. Qian 2026 and Pendse 2025 are relocation proxies, not the measurement. This remains the directory's named empirical hole and the sharpest thing V.3d could commission.
2. **Tessler, Bakker et al., *Science* 2024 (the Habermas Machine primary)** — closed everywhere; only the team's 2026 reflection is on disk, never citable as the findings. Buy/browser list.
3. **Habituation and longitudinal reception.** Every AI-MC result here is single-episode. Nobody has measured what a year of reframed messages does to a receiver's calibration — the question Yang's own limitations section names.
4. **Auditability of the transmutation.** Pariser's invisible-edit constraint has no empirical anchor: no study measures whether receivers can detect, inspect, or contest an interface's reframing, which is the difference between transmutation and quarantine from the inside.
5. **Consent and agency under interface-imposed reframing.** Wang 2021 hints that transmutation works without buy-in; nothing on disk studies whether receivers *want* it, or what withdrawal of the option costs.
6. **Primaries cited but absent:** Richards & Gross 2000 (memory), Butler et al. 2003 (social-partner physiology), Leroy 2009 (attention residue), Toynbee on withdrawal-and-return, and Plato's return to the cave in a citable edition.
7. **Adversarial reception.** Only Tessler §5 touches gaming a mediating intelligence. A transmuting interface is a target; there is no security-side literature here.
8. **Whether a machine in the line suffers** — the model-welfare half of the claim lives in the sibling `model-behavior/` directory, not here; V.3d's clause depends on it.
