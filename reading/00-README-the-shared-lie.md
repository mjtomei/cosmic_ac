# The Shared Lie — reading list

The lineage behind **the shared-lie thesis** (V.3a(e), Matthew, 2026-08-06,
verbatim in `outline-cosmic-ac.md`): one purpose of the belief in "some
orientation towards the good or higher power directly watching you as an
individual" is "to make up for the fact that without the lie, the things that
are like that are **too hard to see for most people**" — and machine counsel
"is a literal instantiation of those things that is **legible to everyone at
any competence level**." The fear it answers: freer information produces
nihilism. And the panopticon worry it must survive, with Matthew's response
(2026-08-06): ⟨M⟩ the worry **requires a power imbalance — which is what the
solution is trying to address**. ⟨/M⟩

**The unifying claim (stated in both directories; Durkheim is the hinge):**
god, king, corporation, and state are the same object — a collective
intelligence personified, with humans as the instantiation medium. The machine
version is the first instantiation that can show its work.

Split 2026-08-07 from `artificial-persons/` at Matthew's direction (that
directory holds the tulpa thesis: doctrine, group agency, cybernetics). This
one holds the experimental-and-meta-analytic half: read it by weighing
evidence, not by following proofs. `notes/` is shared.

## The three clusters

**1. The founding texts.** Plato's noble lie (*Republic* 414b–c — Jowett reads
"royal lie"; and 416d–417b, the guardians' property ban, the oldest office
designed to suppress its occupant); Voltaire's Épître (1769) — "il faudrait
l'inventer," **and the un-quoted passage that is the symmetry answer in 1769:
"Rois, si vous m'opprimez … Mon vengeur est au ciel: apprenez à trembler"** —
the watching god aimed upward at the powerful; Nietzsche, *Gay Science* §125
(the fear in canonical form: loss of reference frame, "Is there still an above
and below?") **and §343** (Nietzsche on the answering side); Durkheim — **the
hinge work with `artificial-persons/`**: the god of the clan *is* the clan
personified, and "the sacred beings, though superior to men, can live only in
the human consciousness."

**2. The watching god, measured — and the corrections.** Norenzayan & Shariff
2008 (the mechanism: salient moralistic deities make anonymity "reputationally
relevant"; the substitution claim — courts, police, contracts "changed the
course of human prosociality"); Purzycki 2016 (a god who knows your *thoughts*;
local-gods null); Shariff & Norenzayan 2007 (secular prime $4.44 ≈ God prime
$4.56 vs control $2.56 — a *visible human-built accountability structure doing
the same work*); Johnson & Krüger 2004 (the omniscient punisher dissolves the
second-order free-rider problem); Northover 2017 (**the meta-analytic zero** on
artificial watching cues); Zuckerman 2009 (secular societies without collapse —
the premise contested honestly). Incoming from the citation graph: the CERC
re-analyses (Bendixen 2023, Lightner 2023 — **read before printing the
4.8×/5.3× figures**), Beheim 2021 (the analysis that forced the Whitehouse
retraction — cite this, never the headline), the priming-replication record
(van Elk 2015, Gomes & McCullough 2015, the Many-Analysts project), Laurin 2012
(supernatural↔institutional monitor substitution, measured), White 2019 (karma:
moral monitoring without a personified god), Watts 2015, Lang 2019
(impartiality inside, hostility outside), and Fitouchi 2025 — **the strongest
live competitor framing**: religions as folk technologies of horizontal mutual
policing, not vertical watching.

**3. The panopticon, and its inversion.** Bentham 1791 primary — Letter I (the
inmate cannot satisfy himself he is *not* watched), Letter VI ("the *apparent
omnipresence* of the Inspector (if divines will allow me the expression)" —
the watching god secularised into architecture, conceded by the designer; and
the doors "thrown wide open … the great *open committee* of the tribunal of the
world" — **the symmetry answer inherited from the panopticon's own author**);
Foucault (the worry citable — "He is seen, but he does not see" — and the
sharpest objection to the symmetry answer: power "automatizes and
disindividualizes"; **if power inheres in the arrangement, universal watching
universalises the mechanism**); Mann-Nolan-Wellman 2003 (sousveillance
field-tested, arguing against its own proposal: asymmetry is *socially*
enforced); Brin's Wired 4.12 essay (the priority date for
the-powerful-become-the-most-watched, 1996).

## The reading path — the shared-lie argument in order
(Entries and numbering from the joint pass.)

**1. `durkheim-1912-elementary-forms.pdf` ★★★**
Start here, not with Hobbes, because Durkheim states the unifying claim as a published sociological thesis rather than an author's invention: the god of the clan *is* the clan, personified. Read Bk. II ch. VII §§I–II and the Conclusion only. §II's reciprocity passage ([Pg 347] — "the sacred beings, though superior to men, can live only in the human consciousness") is the instantiation-medium half of the tulpa structure, in 1912.


### Stage III — Thesis 2: the shared lie, and what replaces it

**10. `voltaire-1769-epitre-trois-imposteurs.pdf` ★★★**
The founding text, in the original, with a real citation (Garnier t. 10, pp. 402–405) instead of the second-hand English everyone else prints. The famous line is there, but the more valuable passage is the one nobody quotes: "Rois, si vous m'opprimez ... Mon vengeur est au ciel : apprenez à trembler." **The watching god aimed upward at the powerful — the paper's symmetry answer already present in 1769.** Correct the filename's date to 1769; supply your own translation or cite a published one.

**11. `nietzsche-gay-science-125-madman.pdf` ★★★**
The fear thesis 2 answers, in canonical form — and note its actual shape: loss of *reference frame*, not of comfort ("Is there still an above and below?"). That maps onto the claim that the orientation structures were always there and the god-belief was the legible proxy. §125 at pp. 167–169; **also read §343 at pp. 275–276**, which puts Nietzsche on the answering side rather than only the fearing side. Pairs directly with Durkheim's "the old gods are growing old or already dead, and others are not yet born." Common's 1910 wording, not Kaufmann's — do not blend.

**12. `norenzayan-shariff-2008-religious-prosociality.pdf` ★★★ [OBJECTION]**
The hinge. Mechanism stated crisply — salient moralistic deities make "an objectively anonymous situation ... reputationally relevant" — and then the substitution claim the paper builds on: courts, policing and contract enforcement "changed the course of human prosociality," with the secular-but-cooperative Northern European datapoint attached. Machine counsel becomes the next member of a series the literature already named. **The objection is inside the same paper:** religious prosociality is "a bounded phenomenon," ingroup-scoped, and "[t]he same mechanisms involved in ingroup altruism may also facilitate outgroup antagonism." Quote that against the thesis, not around it.

**13. `purzycki-2016-moralistic-gods.pdf` ★★★**
The measurement of the exact attribute thesis 2 names — a god who knows your *thoughts*, watching you as an individual. n=591 across eight communities, 35,400 allocations; the local-gods null (ORs 0.96–1.01) is the strongest available answer to omitted-variable objections. Four flags travel with it: the authors call it correlational; their own priming manipulation had no effect; the dependent variable is impartiality toward distant *co-religionists*, i.e. ingroup expansion; Templeton-funded via CERC. Check the post-2016 re-analyses before printing the 4.8×/5.3× figures.

**14. `northover-2017-watching-eyes-meta.pdf` ★★★ [OBJECTION]**
**The batch's hardest counter, and it belongs on the spine.** Two random-effects meta-analyses of artificial surveillance cues on generosity: g = 0.03, CI −0.08 to 0.13 (k=26); log-OR 0.16, CI −0.04 to 0.35 (k=27). This removes the analogical bridge that Shariff & Norenzayan lean on when they argue from eyespots to supernatural watchers, and the moderator chaos they document (Ekström vs Bateson on crowd density, in flat contradiction) is directly usable in the panopticon section. It cuts both ways and the paper should say so: it weakens the claim that a machine watcher automatically produces orientation-toward-good, and it equally weakens the panopticon fear that a symbolic observer coerces behaviour. Scope it honestly — artificial cues, generosity only.

**15. `shariff-norenzayan-2007-god-is-watching.pdf` ★★ *(skippable)***
Read only if the dollar figures are load-bearing. Study 2's three-arm design is the head-to-head the thesis wants: secular prime $4.44 vs God prime $4.56 vs control $2.56 — a reminder of a *visible, human-built* accountability structure doing the same work as the invisible watcher. Also credits the prior claim to Voltaire and Rousseau rather than claiming it. n=50 and n=75, reported d = 1.07 and 0.71, prep, and the exact paradigm Northover meta-analyses to zero. Never print without its contest.

**16. `johnson-kruger-2004-good-of-wrath.pdf` ★★ *(skippable)***
Theory, no new data: why a *watching* god specifically is the functional object — punishment is costly, so enforcement is a second-order public good with its own free-riders, which an omniscient punisher dissolves at zero cost to any believer. Two details earn their keep: surveillance extending to thoughts (Mt. 5:28), which is the panopticon's internalisation claim reached independently, and their framing of people who "subjugate themselves to an illusory threat without 'knowing' it" — the shared lie as cost-benefit, and a clean foil for the point that machine counsel does the same work without the illusion. Take the Voltaire line from Voltaire, not from their note 64.

**17. `zuckerman-2009-atheism-secularity-wellbeing.pdf` ★★ *(skippable)* [OBJECTION]**
Cuts at thesis 2's premise rather than its conclusion: on this evidence, removing the belief leaves no gap needing replacement — secular democracies lead happiness indices and sit at the bottom of homicide rates, and within the US the most religious states are the most violent. It equally refutes the specific fear the paper answers, that freer information yields nihilism. Zuckerman disciplines himself in print (the suicide exception, conceded twice; "correlation is not causation"; atheistic and religious dictatorships both attributed to the dictatorship term), which makes this a contest printable without strawmanning. Every number is borrowed — cite Paul 2005, Norris & Inglehart 2004 directly, and find the published *Sociology Compass* version.

### Stage IV — The panopticon worry and the symmetry answer

**18. `bentham-1791-panopticon.pdf` ★★★**
Go to the primary source, not to Bentham-through-Foucault, for three passages. Letter I p. 3: what matters is not that the inmate is watched but that he cannot satisfy himself he is not. Letter VI p. 28: "the *apparent omnipresence* of the Inspector (if divines will allow me the expression)" — the cluster-4/cluster-5 bridge, the watching god secularised into architecture, with the theological analogy conceded by the designer. Letter VI p. 33: the doors "thrown wide open ... the great *open committee* of the tribunal of the world" — **the symmetry answer as an inheritance from the panopticon's own author.** Image-only scan; transcribe off rasterised pages, cite by Letter and book page.

**19. `foucault-panopticism-chapter.pdf` ★★★ [OBJECTION]**
Three jobs. The worry in its citable form ("He is seen, but he does not see; he is the object of information, never a subject in communication") — state it in Foucault's words before answering it. Then the sharpest objection in the batch: the mechanism "automatizes and disindividualizes power," "it does not matter who exercises power," and the schema's "vocation was to become a generalized function." **If power inheres in the arrangement rather than in the occupant of the tower, universal watching does not dissolve the mechanism — it universalises it.** Thesis 2's symmetry answer must meet that. Third, Foucault himself records Bentham's reciprocity provision (the transparent-building sentence), so the answer can be sourced through the canonical critic. No page numbers in this reprint; quote Bentham from Bentham.

**20. `mann-nolan-wellman-2003-sousveillance.pdf` ★★★ [OBJECTION]**
The only work here that field-tests the symmetry answer, and it argues against its own proposal: "Universal surveillance/sousveillance may, in the end, only serve the ends of the existing dominant power structure" (p. 347). Three findings the paper needs: asymmetry is enforced *socially* — organisational surveillers object to being recorded back, and objection rises with the density of their own cameras; the "will not / may not / cannot" hierarchy, where objections collapse once the sousveiller is visibly acting under external authority, which is uncomfortable for any design hoping symmetry emerges from individual capability; and the accountability sink observed in the field, responsibility "defus[ing] into generalized deferrals to the way things are" (p. 346). Five art performances in one Canadian shopping district — mechanism and objection, never measurement.

**21. `brin-1996-transparent-society-essay.pdf` ★★ *(skippable)***
Close with the priority date. City Two contains the design point in one clause — cameras banned indoors "except Police Headquarters!", where any citizen may watch the control room — i.e. the powerful become the most-watched, published 1996. Skippable because the argument survives on Bentham + Foucault + Mann; its value is attribution, not soundness. Advocacy with no honest limit, and its 1996 press numbers are unsourced. Cite the 1998 book for the argument, this essay for the date and the wording, and say which.

## Side tracks

**A. The priming programme and its collapse.** `shariff-norenzayan-2007` + `johnson-kruger-2004`, read against `northover-2017`. A self-contained bundle: mechanism proposal, the headline experiment, and the meta-analysis that removes its analogical support. Read all three together or none — printing any one alone misrepresents the state of the evidence. Only the meta-analysis is on the spine.


**C. Secularity without a gap.** `zuckerman-2009` alone. A coherent off-spine challenge to thesis 2's *premise* rather than its argument, and the place to go if a reviewer asks what happens when the shared lie simply leaves. Follow its citations out rather than quoting its numbers.


**D. Ancient office design.** `plato-republic-jowett` 416d–417b, read next to Dartmouth and Beer rather than with the noble-lie material. The guardians' property ban is the oldest statement in the directory of an office built to suppress the person filling it. The 414b passage is the shared-lie thesis's named ancestor but functions as its **foil** — Plato's fiction is top-down, total, and must not be inspected, which is the exact inversion of a counsel that shows its work. Quote the framing sentence only, never the myth's autochthony-and-caste content, and never call it the "noble lie" while quoting Jowett, whose text reads "royal lie."

### (c) Revised reading path — 29 on the spine, 6 off it

Same argument structure as the 2026-08-06 path (the unifying claim → the lie and
the fear → the god measured → the panopticon and the answer), rebuilt around the
works that were missing when it was written. **Objections sit next to the thing
they contest, never in an appendix.** The single ★ is off the path; so are four
★★s whose argument is now carried better by something else.

#### Stage I — the unifying claim

**1. `durkheim-1912-elementary-forms.pdf` ★★★**
Unchanged, and still first: the god of the clan *is* the clan, personified, as a
published sociological thesis rather than an author's invention. Bk. II ch. VII
§§I–II and the Conclusion. [Pg 347], "the sacred beings, though superior to men,
can live only in the human consciousness."

#### Stage II — the lie named, and the fear it answers

**2. `voltaire-1769-epitre-trois-imposteurs.pdf` ★★★**
The founding text in the original. The famous line, and the un-quoted passage
that is the symmetry answer in 1769: "Rois, si vous m'opprimez … Mon vengeur est
au ciel : apprenez à trembler." The watching god aimed *upward*.

**3. `nietzsche-gay-science-125-madman.pdf` ★★★**
The fear in canonical form, and note its shape — loss of *reference frame*, not
of comfort. §125 pp. 167–169, §343 pp. 275–276. Common 1910; never blend with
Kaufmann.

#### Stage III — the watching god, measured

*III.a — the framework, stated by its authors*

**4. `norenzayan-2014-bbs-target.pdf` ★★★ ⚠ — NEW ENTRY POINT**
This replaces the Science review as the door into cluster 2, because everything
downstream is a test of it or an attack on it and here the attacks ship in the
same file: target article, 27 open commentaries, authors' response. Read the
target, then §R1.1 ("Things that we did not say"), then the commentaries that
match the objections you already hold. The one fact to carry out: footnote 8,
added after acceptance, re-runs the priming meta-analysis with Gomes &
McCullough included and finds the effect positive for believers (g = 0.38,
p = .002) and absent for nonbelievers (g = 0.12, p = .31). **The interface only
works on people who already hold it.** Cite as BBS 39 (2016), e1, not 2014.

**5. `norenzayan-shariff-2008-religious-prosociality.pdf` ★★★ ⚠**
Still on the spine, now as the four-page form of the same argument, and read for
one thing the long version states less crisply: the substitution claim — courts,
policing and contract enforcement "changed the course of human prosociality."
Machine counsel joins a series the literature already named. The objection is
inside the paper: religious prosociality is "a bounded phenomenon," and the same
mechanisms "may also facilitate outgroup antagonism."

*III.b — the individual-level measurements, and the CERC self-audit*

**6. `purzycki-2016-moralistic-gods.pdf` ★★★**
The measurement of the exact attribute the thesis names — a god who knows your
*thoughts*. n = 591, 35,400 allocations; the local-gods null (ORs 0.96–1.01) is
still the best answer to omitted-variable objections. Correlational; its own
priming manipulation did nothing; the DV is impartiality toward distant
*co-religionists*.

**7. `lang-2019-moralizing-gods-parochialism.pdf` ★★**
Wave II, 2,228 participants, 15 societies, read immediately after 6 because it is
the same instrument auditing itself. Impartiality replicates; **every** outgroup
prediction and **every** priming manipulation fails. Reward ratings do nothing —
only monitoring-and-punishing predicts, which keeps the mechanism about
surveillance. And keep the participants-section sentence: two RAs at one site
counterfeited data, 72 participants dropped.

**8. `bendixen-2023-cerc-retest.pdf` ★★**
The pre-registered CERC re-test, and the most precise result in the cluster for
this directory's purposes: whether the god is *angered by immorality* does not
predict cooperative play, while punitiveness and knowledge-breadth main effects
stay positive. Read as a bandwidth measurement, not a debunking — what crosses
the interface is *that something sees and answers*, not the schedule of offences
it cares about. Not independent of 6 and 7; it is those data re-interrogated.

*III.c — the historical record, and its retraction*

**9. `beheim-2021-moralizing-gods-reanalysis.pdf` ★★★ ⚠**
The Matters Arising that forced the *Nature* retraction. Six pages; take the
mechanism, not the gossip: 61% of observations were "Unknown," recoded to
"absent" at a named line of the authors' own scripts, giving r = 0.97 between
missingness and coded absence — "the study can be viewed as an analysis of the
missingness patterns in the Seshat database." The portable principle is the
directory's own: **a watching god is attested only where someone was writing, so
every dataset of divine surveillance is first a dataset of the surveillance of
the record.**

**10. `whitehouse-2022-retake.pdf` ★★★ ⚠ — never read 9 without this**
The watched party's answer, and the strongest single objection on the shelf. The
error is conceded and repaired (z falls 6.8 → 5.0); the headline survives; and
the rebuilt causal analysis then rejects the Big Gods hypothesis from the other
direction — the complexity/religion correlation is attributed to shared drivers
(interpolity competition, cavalry, agricultural productivity). §4 is the part
this directory needs most: retraction as annulment rather than amendment, and
what happens when the corrections record is itself asymmetric in who is watching
whom. Self-interested, and it says so.

**11. `purzycki-2022-methods-critique.pdf` ★★ ⚠**
The reply to 10, placed with it. The escalation claim: the retracted paper
substituted absence for missingness in the *analysis*, where a critic could read
the script; the repair moved the substitution into the *database*, where 463 of
471 coded absences are now inferred and upstream of every future user. The DAG +
1,000-run simulation is the rigorous part — the estimate moves *further* from the
truth when the confounder is held constant. A commentary on a target article the
directory does not hold; same camp as 9.

**12. `lightner-2023-moralistic-punishment-complexity.pdf` ★★ ⚠**
Now indict the *other* database. The SCCS "moralizing high gods" variable (V237)
requires a creator or director of the universe, which filters out ordinary
punitive gods; the false negatives cluster at low complexity; a simulation over
10,000 societies with no true association recovers a significant positive one.
If it holds, Swanson, Roes & Raymond, Johnson 2005 and Botero all inherit the
artifact. §4 is the directory-critical part — the missionary sources were written
by people whose interest was in finding an absence.

**13. `watts-2015-broad-supernatural-punishment.pdf` ★★★ ⚠**
The payload of the whole sub-stage, and the reason to read III.c at all. Fix
Galton's problem with a Bayesian phylogeny over 96 Austronesian cultures and the
arrow reverses: *broad* supernatural punishment — ancestral spirits, impersonal
karma-like processes — precedes political complexity; the moralizing high god
follows it, diffused as high-status packaging. **That separates the watcher from
the supreme person, which is exactly the claim that the god is a low-bandwidth
interface to a real structure and not the structure.** Thin base (6 of 96),
modest Bayes factors, Austronesia only.

*III.d — the experimental record and its collapse*

**14. `shariff-norenzayan-2007-god-is-watching.pdf` ★★ — promoted onto the spine**
No longer skippable, because it is now the *object* of the next two. Study 2's
three-arm design is still the head-to-head the thesis wants: secular prime $4.44
≈ God prime $4.56 vs control $2.56 — a visible human-built accountability
structure doing the same work as the invisible watcher. n = 50 and n = 75. Read
it, then immediately read what happened to it.

**15. `gomes-mccullough-2015-preregistered-null.pdf` ★★★ ⚠**
The preregistered N = 650 direct replication of that experiment, at 13× the cell
size, with the original materials supplied by the original authors. Nothing:
$4.49 / $4.28 / $4.70, no moderation by anything. Two extras earn its rank —
93% of participants recalled the "implicit" religious words, so the paradigm was
never subliminal; and the control baseline was already at 45% of the endowment,
which is the ceiling that item 18 will use to explain the null. Preregistration
was partial (26% of subjects already run); say so.

**16. `vanelk-2015-priming-metaanalysis.pdf` ★★ ⚠**
The meta-analysis behind item 4 re-analysed two ways, returning −0.002 by
PET-PEESE and ~0.3 by Bayesian bias correction — and the authors decline to pick.
Cite it for the claim it makes (meta-analysis cannot adjudicate a disputed effect
built from non-preregistered studies), never as "the effect is zero." Keep the
power arithmetic: at g = 0.18 you need 766 participants, and exactly one study in
92 cleared that bar.

**17. `northover-2017-watching-eyes-meta.pdf` ★★★ ⚠**
Still the hardest counter, and still on the spine: artificial being-watched cues
do nothing to generosity (g = 0.03, CI −0.08 to 0.13). It removes the analogical
bridge from eyespots to supernatural watchers — and it cuts both ways, weakening
the panopticon fear as much as the thesis. Scope honestly: artificial cues,
generosity only. Item 24 will explain part of the divergence.

*III.e — what survives the nulls*

**18. `white-2019-karma.pdf` ★★★**
The best-evidenced positive result in the directory, and the answer to III.d
rather than a retreat from it. Four pre-registered high-powered experiments
(N = 3,591), multiverse-checked. Two payloads: **karma works** — an impersonal,
non-agentic process enforces norms as well as a person-shaped god, which is the
shared-lie thesis in data (what does the work is that the structure keeps
accounts, not that anyone is looking); and it *explains* item 15 rather than
dismissing it — the effect exists only among initially selfish givers, and a 45%
control baseline has nowhere to move. Post hoc, and all four samples American.
It also volunteers a null against itself (no karmic payback).

**19. `fitouchi-2025-folk-technologies.pdf` ★★★ ⚠ — close cluster 2 here**
The strongest live competitor framing, and the one that survives everything in
III.c–III.d because it never needed the god to work: people endorse the watching
god because they believe it makes *others* cooperate. That is the shared-lie
thesis inverted into evolutionary psychology — the interface has to be
*believed-in* by the people you are policing, not accurate. Its secularization
section derives decline from rising social trust reducing the perceived necessity
of the belief, which is the directly testable form of the claim that a real
observability structure can replace the proxy. Prediction 3b (religion polices
what the state cannot reach) is the panopticon question in historical dress.
Accepted MS; Baumard/Boyer camp, i.e. an interested party.

#### Stage IV — the panopticon worry, the symmetry answer, and its measurement

**20. `bentham-1791-panopticon.pdf` ★★★**
Primary source, three passages. Letter I p. 3 (the inmate cannot satisfy himself
he is *not* watched); Letter VI p. 28 ("the *apparent omnipresence* of the
Inspector (if divines will allow me the expression)" — the bridge from Stage III,
conceded by the designer); Letter VI p. 33 (the doors "thrown wide open … the
great *open committee* of the tribunal of the world" — the symmetry answer
inherited from the panopticon's own author). Image-only scan; transcribe off
rasterised pages, cite by Letter.

**21. `foucault-panopticism-chapter.pdf` ★★★ ⚠**
The worry in citable form ("He is seen, but he does not see"), then the sharpest
objection: the mechanism "automatizes and disindividualizes power," "it does not
matter who exercises power." **If power inheres in the arrangement rather than in
the occupant, universal watching universalises the mechanism rather than
dissolving it.** Foucault also records Bentham's reciprocity provision, so the
answer can be sourced through the critic.

**22. `laurin-2012-outsourcing-punishment.pdf` ★★ ⚠**
Placed here, not in Stage III, because it contests the panopticon's *premise*
that an observer is a net addition. Believing in a powerful intervening God
*reduces* costly third-party punishment and support for state punishment, via
the attribution that punishing is God's job — while general religiosity raises
both, so "religion" as an aggregate is the wrong unit. Ported to this directory:
a legible always-on observer plausibly crowds out the diffuse mutual watching it
was meant to formalise. Read as hypothesis, not evidence — N = 20 to 76,
interactions at p = .03–.05, 2012 vintage, exactly the design class III.d gutted.

**23. `okumura-2023-robot-watching-reputation.pdf` ★★**
The only work here where the watcher is an actual machine, so it is the empirical
hinge for machine counsel as the legible instantiation. Read it for its nulls:
eyes did nothing, motion and speech did nothing, attributed intelligence did
nothing. What produced reputation management was *prior social interaction* —
being treated as an evaluator with a mind, not being surveilled. **An observer
that is merely present and capable is not felt; one that has previously answered
you is.** n = 28 per cell, unregistered, one lab, one age, and the attribution
measure was run on different children.

**24. `bradley-2018-real-observability.pdf` ★★★ ⚠ — the quantitative spine**
117 papers, n = 788,164, and a three-way distinction that maps onto this
directory exactly: *perceived* observability (sensed watcher, nothing actually
revealed — the god) r = 0.100; *pseudo* (behaviour revealed, identity masked)
r = 0.167; *overt* (identity and behaviour revealed — machine counsel's case)
r = 0.172. The low-bandwidth interface works, but least. The two honest
moderators matter more than the headline: lab beats field almost 2:1 (0.170 vs
0.077), and effects grow only with consequences (0.248 vs 0.119) and repeated
interaction (0.269 vs 0.106) — observability alone does little; observability
plus a live reputational ledger does the work. It also reconciles itself with
item 17 rather than ignoring it. **Always quote r = 0.141 with I² = 99.24
attached, and never report the perceived/overt gradient as established — the
omnibus test across manipulation types was not significant (p = 0.167).**

**25. `mann-nolan-wellman-2003-sousveillance.pdf` ★★★ ⚠**
The only work that field-tests the inversion, arguing against its own proposal:
"Universal surveillance/sousveillance may, in the end, only serve the ends of the
existing dominant power structure" (p. 347). Three findings: asymmetry is
enforced *socially*, and objection rises with the density of the objector's own
cameras; the "will not / may not / cannot" hierarchy, where objections collapse
once the sousveiller is visibly acting under external authority; and the
accountability sink, responsibility "defus[ing] into generalized deferrals to the
way things are" (p. 346). Five performances in one shopping district — mechanism,
never measurement.

**26. `brin-1998-transparent-society-ch1.pdf` ★★**
The symmetry answer in its author's voice, and chapter 1 is where the whole
argument is made: the two cities, accountability as the precondition of privacy
rather than its opposite, the restaurant analogy ("open visibility seems to
favor defense over offense"), and the behavioural law that makes symmetry
structural rather than voluntary — "whenever a conflict appears between privacy
and accountability, people demand the former for themselves and the latter for
everybody else." **Read it for the normative structure and never for the
evidence:** its King's Lynn and Glasgow crime numbers are uncited trade-book
anecdote, and items 27–29 are the corrective. Its own 1998 forecast is worth
recording as a tell — the world since has mostly built city one.

**27. `lum-2020-bwc-review.pdf` ★★★ ⚠**
The corrective, at Campbell standard: 30 studies, 116 effects, on the largest
observability intervention ever actually deployed. Use of force −6.8% ns;
assaults on officers +15.9% ns; arrests, self-initiated activity, dispatched
calls all ns. One significant result — complaints −16.6% — and the authors refuse
to interpret it, because it is equally consistent with better behaviour and with
citizens concluding it is pointless to complain when the officer holds the tape.
**That ambiguity is the panopticon worry restated as a measurement problem: an
observability regime can produce a metric that looks like accountability and is
actually silence.** The defensible claim is not "BWCs do nothing" but "the
deployed configuration does not reliably do what was promised, and we cannot yet
say when it does."

**28. `yokum-2019-dc-bwc-rct.pdf` ★★ ⚠**
The cleanest single null, and the one that closes the escape hatches: 2,224
officers, *individual*-level randomization, preregistered, two independent
analysis teams, compliance verified (665 videos/year vs 14). Force +74 per 1,000
(SE 87), complaints +57 (SE 41) — nothing, in all seven districts. Its opening
paragraph builds the bridge explicitly from the lab literature this directory
runs on (Hawthorne, Haley & Fessler, Bateson, Gerber, Nettle) to the
highest-stakes field deployment, and the bridge does not carry. Then it works
through spillover, ambient cameras, nonadherence and unreported force one at a
time. **This is what the symmetry answer has to carry honestly.**

**29. `ariel-2017-bwc.pdf` ★★ ⚠**
End on the mechanism, not the magnitude. Being watched *backfires*: 37% higher
odds of assault on camera shifts, worst where officers had least discretion over
activation — "over-deterrence," built on the same self-awareness theory that
makes the watching god a regulator, run past its optimum. Two portable
micro-mechanisms: *politeness read as weakness*, and *skipping stages on the
force continuum* because the intermediate move looks bad on tape — the best
statement anywhere here of the claim that legible observation deforms behaviour
by pricing appearance rather than outcome. Cite the mechanism only: item 27 pools
this construct to non-significance and notes that Ariel's ten anonymous sites are
most of the evidence for Ariel's result; the group's own "93% reduction in
complaints" claim lands at ~17% in the meta-analysis, which is a calibration
warning about the source.
