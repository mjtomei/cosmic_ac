## I. The counsel gap stated (open with these — Matthew: Lippmann intros the idea)

1. ★★★ **lippmann-1922-public-opinion.pdf** — the diagnosis: citizens act on
   "pictures in our heads" of a "pseudo-environment"; the world is out of reach,
   out of sight, out of mind. The counsel gap named, 1922.
2. ★★★ **lippmann-1925-phantom-public.pdf** — the harder conclusion: the
   omnicompetent citizen is a phantom; govern by insiders, monitored weakly.
3. ★★★ **dewey-1927-public-and-its-problems.pdf** — the reply that is our
   thesis statement: the cure is not shrinking the demos but improving the
   means of inquiry and communication. Machine intelligence is Dewey's answer,
   a century late.
