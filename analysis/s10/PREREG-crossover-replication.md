# Pre-registration: II-over-I crossover replication in nine new chambers

**Frozen 2026-08-16, before any covariate collection for these chambers.**
Purpose: the next expansion round is CONFIRMATORY. Everything below is fixed
now so that no analytic choice can be made after seeing the new data.

## Hypothesis

Within a legislature, class II members (lower service: teachers, nurses,
journalists, lower administrators) use the Kobak style-word register at a
higher rate than class I members (higher service: lawyers, physicians,
professors, higher administrators), adjusted for birth cohort.

## Chambers

The nine corpus-held chambers currently lacking covariates: NSW, QLD, VIC,
WA, SA, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland, New Brunswick. A chamber is
testable if it yields >= 8 members in each of classes I and II.

## Protocol, fixed

1. Covariates collected by the established workflow (roster from corpus
   speaker keys via FW.norm; occupation strings verbatim; double-blind EGP
   coding on the v2 rubric with adjudication; agreement rate reported).
2. Per chamber: cohort-adjusted II - I contrast in mean member-level career
   rate (equal member weight, >= 8,000 words per member), exactly as in the
   2026-08-16 within-chamber table.
3. Combined: (a) sign test across testable new chambers; (b) inverse-
   variance meta of the new chambers alone; (c) updated all-chamber meta.
4. NO alternative specifications. The z-scored regressions may be run for
   context but adjudicate nothing.

## What counts as what

- REPLICATION: new-chamber meta positive with z >= 1.96, or >= 7 of 9 signs
  positive (binomial p < 0.09).
- INFORMATIVE NON-REPLICATION: powered chambers flat/negative — reported as
  a scope finding (which legislatures carry the crossover), not suppressed.
- The existing 12-chamber result stands on its own either way; this round
  tests GENERALITY.
