==============================================================================
READING
==============================================================================
  THE TENURE SLOPE IS NOT NULL. On the strict cell -- same birth YEAR,
  same spoken year, same chamber, so age, period and cohort are all
  exactly held -- a decade of extra service is worth -0.951 per 1,000
  words (t -8.3 clustered on member; bootstrap CI excludes 0).
  That is the same order of magnitude as §4.6's entire birth gradient
  (+0.876 per decade) and as the pre-drift Senate age slope (-0.892).
  By the decision rule this test was set up with, that outcome favours
  the CAREER-STAGE rival, not the cohort reading.

  BUT READ THE CONTRAST CORRECTLY. Within a (birth year x year x
  chamber) cell each member appears at most once -- verified above --
  so this is a purely BETWEEN-member comparison. It cannot be members
  drifting down as they serve. Indeed within member, tenure and calendar
  year move one-for-one (entry is fixed), so a within-member tenure
  slope does not exist at all: it is the APC problem again one level
  down, tenure/period/entry in place of age/period/cohort. And
  cohort_vs_period.py's within-member trend is POSITIVE (+0.51 per
  decade): sitting members' register rises. So the negative tenure slope
  is a statement about WHO ARRIVED WHEN, not about what service does to
  a person.

  WHICH MEANS THIS IS NOT A CLEAN TEST OF THE RIVAL. With year, chamber
  and birth year all held, tenure is exactly minus entry year, so the
  slope reported here is the ENTRY-COHORT gradient with a sign flip --
  it reproduces cohort_vs_period.py's three-stamp entry term (+1.055 per
  decade of entry on these same 37,548 rows). §4.6's own footnote calls
  entry year 'a second cohort clock, not a confounder'. Both readings
  fit this number, and nothing in this design separates them: a
  career-stage effect and an entry-cohort effect are the same
  coefficient. What the test does establish is that the number is large,
  and that §4.6 cannot claim tenure is null.

  CAVEAT: censoring drops 4,254 members and truncates the long-tenure
  tail at each chamber's coverage edge, so the slope is fitted mostly
  over 1-15 years of service.
