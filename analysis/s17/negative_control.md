# S17 negative control — imitation lags where machine intelligence is not the mechanism

Verified 2026-08-04 (web agents, primary sources where stated). The control
the study design requires: in domains where the imitator is not a machine,
the imitation lag has not collapsed — it is flat over 25–45 years while the
AI-sector lag fell from ~23 months to 3–4 months.

## The comparison table

| Domain | Old lag | Recent lag | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| US small-molecule drugs, brand launch → first generic entry | 13.5 yr (1995–96 entries) | 14.1 yr all NMEs / 13.0 yr >$250M (2017–19 entries) | flat to slightly up |
| US biosimilars, approval → launch | — | 34 mo US vs 7.4 mo Canada / 4.7 mo UK (2022) | no collapse; US lag is institutional |
| Leading-edge logic fab, leader → follower volume production | ~4 yr (28nm: TSMC 2011 → SMIC ~2015) | ~4 yr (7nm: TSMC 2018 → SMIC 2022); ~5 yr (ITIF 2024 aggregate) | flat |
| Manufacturing generally (Mansfield's own object) | imitation cost ~65% / time ~70%, 60% imitated within 4 yr (1981) | **never remeasured** | unmeasured for 45 years |

## Sources, per row

**Pharma (the strongest series).** Grabowski, Long & Mortimer, *J Med Econ*
2013 (doi:10.3111/13696998.2013.873723), Table 1 read from full text: average
market-exclusivity period (launch → first generic) 13.5 yr for 1995–96
entries, 12.9 yr for 2011–12, "relatively constant over the past 10–15
years." Update: Grabowski, Long, Mortimer & Bilginsoy, *J Med Econ*
24(1):908–917, 2021 (doi:10.1080/13696998.2021.1952795): 2017–19 entries,
14.1 yr all NMEs / 13.0 yr >$250M — "changed relatively little over the past
decade." Stability corroborated by Hemphill & Sampat, *J Health Econ*
31(2):327–339, 2012 (doi:10.1016/j.jhealeco.2012.01.004): effective market
life "has hardly changed over the decade" (their ~12.2-yr mean is body-text,
verified only via secondary — pull PDF if load-bearing).
*Caveat to state:* the pharma lag is legally constructed (Hatch-Waxman), not
a pure technical imitation lag. That is the point stated honestly: chemically
duplicating a small molecule is trivial and entry still takes 12–14 years —
in this domain the binding constraint is institutional, and it has not moved.

**Biosimilars.** Goode & Chao, *J Law & the Biosciences* 9(2):lsac022, 2022
(doi:10.1093/jlb/lsac022): mean approval→launch delay US 34 mo vs Canada 7.4
/ UK 4.7; patents asserted US 16.2 vs 1.8/1.3; Humira US portfolio 73
patents, "80% non-patentably distinct." Drake & Conti, *JHPPL* 2026
(doi:10.1215/03616878-12513812): biosimilar effective market life comparable
to small-molecule generics through 2025. Single-drug originator→biosimilar
anchors (Humira 2003→2023, ~20 yr) are correct as facts but no peer-reviewed
mean exists — use only as flagged illustrations.

**Semiconductors.** TSMC 28nm volume production 2011-10-24 (TSMC PR); SMIC
14nm mass production Q4-2019 (SMIC Q4-2019 results, 2020-02-13); TSMC N7
volume 2018-04; SMIC 7nm found in silicon 2022-07-20 (TechInsights teardown).
ITIF 2024-08-19: SMIC "about five years behind." *Caveats:* node names are
marketing labels (TechInsights argues the 7nm pair is genuinely comparable);
export controls confound the post-2020 half and INFLATE the recent lag — but
the pre-control pairs (2011→2015) show the same ~4 yr, which is the
defensible form: the lag never collapsed, controls or no controls.
TechInsights/ITIF are not peer-reviewed; the company dates are primary.

**The Mansfield absence.** Hall, Helmers, Rogers & Sena, *JEL* 52(2):375–423,
2014, Table 3 spans the entire survey tradition (Yale 1987 → Carnegie Mellon
2000 → CIS): all of it measures *perceived effectiveness* of appropriability
mechanisms; none measures imitation time or cost. Cohen, Nelson & Walsh 2000
(NBER WP 7552, read from the primary PDF) contains no imitation-lag
measurement at all — it shows lead time and secrecy as the top mechanisms
(product lead time 52.8; secrecy ~51; patents 34.8), i.e., a durable lead
still exists to be relied on. **No modern replication of Mansfield's
imitation-cost/time survey exists.** Closest is Giachetti & Lanzolla, *LRP*
2016 (UK handsets, 1997–2008) — paywalled, numbers unverified, do not quote.
Per the repo rule: state this absence at most once, quietly, never rest
weight on it.
