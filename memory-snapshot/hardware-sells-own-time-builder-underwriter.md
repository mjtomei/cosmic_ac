Matthew's idea (2026-07-20), INSERTED into §11(2) + Appendix A (commit 9a3a6ea):

- **Hardware built to be cheaper by selling its own free time** — participation is an architectural fact below the OS (firmware/hypervisor/silicon), where security guarantees are strongest (precedent: TrustZone, SEV, Nitro-class isolation; seL4 proved kernel — Klein et al., SOSP 2009). This is the *best version of Omerta*; the software version is merely what can be built first without a fab (appendix carries this framing per the main-text-published-only rule).
- **The barrier is not just programmability but integration into society**: sharing must get past gatekeepers and self-interested individuals who price it as unnecessary risk (the §5 career-risk arithmetic recursed).
- **The counter is financing + insurance (Matthew's refinement, 2026-07-20, commit 8002087)**: a flat discount is too weak for ~30% utilization and forces ex-ante pricing. Instead: **0%-interest device loan, monthly payment varies with compute shared — down to NEGATIVE (profit-sharing)**, so the instrument de-risks households at one end and mints farm-building providers at the other. Consumer entry (households price their own tradeoff; gatekeepers price career risk). Precedents: solar third-party financing, carrier-subsidized handsets. Plus builder insures residual risk (warranty as unfakeable quality signal — Grossman 1981, canonical-from-knowledge, flagged).
- **Builder as ultimate underwriter**: extends §10's vendor triad (vantage/channel/incentive) with liability — the builder built the isolation so only it can price it; a builder made responsible for trusting its own machine has the incentive to build a machine worth trusting.

**Omerta roadmap derived from the instrument (Matthew 2026-07-20, in Appendix A, commit 7eeb8de):** Phase 1 — network software on OUR OWN consumer devices, selling inference/services ("a convoluted services business"): proves consumer-silicon-serves-paid-work permission-free AND measures the utilization/demand/reliability distributions a lender needs. Phase 2 — financial services: free/0%-financed devices, purchased compute reduces the monthly payment through zero into profit-sharing. Phase 3 — the hardware endgame. Each phase de-risks the next; none needs a gatekeeper.
